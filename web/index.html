CSET225 syllabus

Spatial sampling
Downsample a known reference and reconstruct at 2× or 4×.
Intensity transformations
Apply histogram equalization, CLAHE, or gamma correction.
Spatial filtering
Apply Gaussian or median smoothing before inference.
Edge detection
Compare Canny and Sobel maps on matched crops.
Color processing
Inspect the luminance and chroma channels in YCbCr.
Frequency-domain filtering
View 2D DFT spectra and try Gaussian low-pass or high-boost.
Compression and fidelity
Simulate JPEG degradation, then measure RGB PSNR and SSIM.
Video processing
Upscale sampled frames and export H.264 video with source audio.
